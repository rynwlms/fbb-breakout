Vibe Coder Exercise: Build, Ship, Collaborate

Build and publish a very small web-based game.

This exercise is not about game design. Keep the game simple. We are evaluating delivery, judgment, problem-solving, and collaboration more than product ambition.

You are not expected to already know every platform, permission model, or deployment workflow involved. Part of the exercise is demonstrating how you close knowledge gaps, make pragmatic decisions, and get to a working result.

Use AI like it’s your unfair advantage. Because it is. We are not evaluating how much you can do manually. We are evaluating how effectively you use AI, what you choose to verify, and whether you stay in control of the result.

Requirements

By the end of the exercise, you should deliver:

A small browser-based game
Local high score persistence that survives refreshes in the same browser
A public deployment we can access and play
A code repository that you administer and structure for shared development
Basic contributor instructions so other developers can clone the repo, make a change, and submit a PR
Review of two PRs submitted by our team:
accept one and merge it
reject one with a clear explanation of what must change for acceptance
deploy the accepted change
Optional extension

A global high score is optional, but will materially strengthen a submission if executed well.

For this exercise, “global” means the score is stored outside the browser in a shared backend or hosted data store, so multiple users on different devices or browsers can interact with the same score data.

Timeline

Day 1

Build and ship the initial version. Send:

live URL
repo URL
Your repo should include:

README.md with local setup and contribution instructions
DECISIONS_AND_OBSTACLES.md explaining scope, key decisions, friction encountered, and tradeoffs made
AI_USAGE.md explaining how you used AI, where it helped, and what you verified yourself
Day 2

We will provide two team members for collaboration. You should grant access and ensure each person can contribute via branch + PR.

You will not be penalized for delays on our side related to scheduling, access acceptance, or responsiveness.

Day 3

Review both PRs, reject one with clear feedback, accept and merge one, and deploy the accepted change.

What we are evaluating

We care most about:

your ability to get to a working result
your ability to overcome unfamiliar technical obstacles
your judgment in choosing tools and managing complexity
your ability to create a usable collaboration workflow
your ability to explain what you did and why
Pragmatism beats ambition. Shipping beats polish.
